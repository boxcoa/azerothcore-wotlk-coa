title: Inn-Sane's Rest buff restores no mana outside a rest area
---
Fix #11278: in the Hardcore - Inn-Sane trials, the Rest buff gained while eating or drinking no longer restores
2% of maximum mana every 2 seconds outside a rest area. Inside an inn it restores mana as before. The same
applies to any percentage power tick for a challenger whose rules forbid energizing.
