title: Experience boosts help new alts catch up to your highest character
---
On an account with no level 60 or prestiged character, a character below the level of another character
on the account can now use Potion of Experience and Aura of Experience. When it reaches that character's
level, its active boosts are stored in the Aetherial Reliquary, the same way they are at max level. The
Reliquary restores them once the character is below the account's highest level again.

Accounts that already have a level 60 or prestiged character keep the current rules. A character alone
on an account without one still cannot use the boosts.
